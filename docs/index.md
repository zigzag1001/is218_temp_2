# Career Research and Portfolio Plan

This is the **career research site** for the IS218-003 Portfolio Plan, not a finished personal portfolio. It is separate from the AI/programming research site for the creative game.

## Question and audience

**Question:** What do selected forward-deployed and AI software roles ask engineers to do, and how should a student portfolio show relevant, verifiable work?

**Intended portfolio reader:** a recruiter or engineering reviewer looking for an early-career developer's projects, actual contributions, and evidence of how the work was checked. This is a working audience assumption, not a claim about a real reader's feedback.

## Explore

- [Original employer sources](employers.md): three role descriptions, dated observations, requirements, and limits.
- [Portfolio plan](portfolio-plan.md): public-facing career directions, design alternatives, chosen reader, and checkable stories.
- [Peer review and submission](review.md): the planned walkthrough, issue/retry record, and short submission outline.
